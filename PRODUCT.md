## Audience

Hiring managers and technical collaborators assessing Amanda for computer vision, ML engineering, and applied AI roles.

## Purpose

Demonstrate visual understanding through several representations of one photograph, supported by clear project evidence and Amanda's product and leadership perspective.

## Approved direction

Sleek, image-led portfolio. The hero is real Blender-built geometry rendered interactively in WebGL. The corrected direction is scene understanding, not a stack of artistic photograph cutouts.

The same source image, `las-vegas.jpeg`, supports RGB pixels, object detection, semantic segmentation, relative depth, a depth-derived 3D surface, and a point cloud. Roads, vehicles, and vegetation can be inspected separately using model-derived classes. Dark graphite, cool white, restrained cyan, and precise typography connect the rest of the website.

## Visitor paths

- Explore image representations and highlight semantic categories.
- Read the project stories, education, recognition, and About narrative directly.
- Contact Amanda or read the public résumé without operating the scene.
- Inspect the inference provenance and download the editable Blender scene.

## Content controls

Use Amanda's supplied About text and previously confirmed project facts. The inference models are pretrained third-party models; this hero demonstrates their use and representation, not a claim that Amanda trained them. Predictions are precomputed locally, not live browser inference. Confidence scores are actual model outputs and are not accuracy measurements.

Keep 96% specific to fruit classification. Preserve 75% administrative workload reduction and 25% booking growth with their context. Copilot efficiency improvements remain intended, not measured. The SFU reconstruction contribution remains qualified pending clarification. CanAI internal architecture and data stay private. Keep the public résumé and contact details unchanged.

## Boundaries

No telemetry, image upload, hosted inference API, production deployment, commit, or pull request is part of this change. The models run locally to generate the assets; their weights are excluded from version control. No pipeline output is hand-corrected to look more accurate.

Estimated depth has relative scale and assumed camera geometry. A single image does not establish metric dimensions, occluded surfaces, or a complete reconstruction.

## Portfolio flow

The page introduces Amanda, followed by About, Experience, Projects,
and Contact. Azure certification appears within About. The interactive
pothole detection demo belongs within the pothole project.

A decorative translucent tube connects the page sections. Skill logos
automatically flow downward in staggered loops without requiring scrolling.

see a stationary pile.

The layout follows Swiss-inspired minimalism: a white background,
charcoal text, restrained teal, one system sans-serif family, consistent
alignment, generous spacing, and concrete first-person copy.
