Computer vision / SFU / 2025

Reading the road
beneath the surface.

Pothole detection & relative depth estimation

I combined YOLOv8 detection with MiDaS monocular depth estimation to distinguish potholes from the surrounding road.

The pipeline brings together bounding-box region analysis, depth normalization, and noise filtering across varied lighting, textures, and camera perspectives.

  • YOLOv8
  • MiDaS
  • Region analysis

Relative depth compares the scene; it is not a measurement in centimetres. Visual is a scene study, not project output.

As a Computer Vision Engineer, I combine technical development, product thinking, and leadership to turn complex challenges into practical, responsible AI solutions.
